# ZonaryOS

**A universal, sector-agnostic ERP/WMS/MES system kernel.**

ZonaryOS is designed so that any business — regardless of industry or scale, from a corner store to an aircraft factory — can sign up on its own, with no outside help, and meet its entire end-to-end operational needs: accounting, inventory, production, logistics, and its relationship with its own customers, all from a single, fully parametric core.

## Status

🚧 **Early development.** Building a first, narrow, end-to-end vertical slice (single firm, simple inventory + sale flow). See [`docs/VISION.md`](docs/VISION.md) for the full product vision and architecture decisions.
